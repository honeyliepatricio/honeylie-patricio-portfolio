This folder is where your site images live.

Add your professional photo here as:

  profile.jpg

Recommended: a portrait-orientation photo, at least 800x1000px, well-lit,
professional attire. The homepage hero references it at /images/profile.jpg
(see src/lib/site.ts -> profileImage).

Until you add a real photo, the browser will show a broken-image icon in
that spot, since no file exists yet at that path. Add your real photo
before deploying so visitors see your photo instead.
